## Supplied reference and generated illustration

- `priya-reference.png`: user-supplied portrait, originally named `image-1791475143468.png`; likeness reference for the illustrated character.
- `priya-camera.*`: generated stylized character based on that portrait, including a background-cleanup revision.
- `priya-coffee.*`: generated alternate pose using the character and supplied portrait as references.
- `courtyard.*`: generated imaginative South Indian courtyard. It is not a photograph of Kandavalli or a painting made by Priya.
- `autumn.*`: generated Oneonta/upstate New York-inspired regional scene, not an exact geographic depiction or a painting made by Priya.

Full available prompts are preserved in `ASSET-PROMPTS.txt`. No verified photographs of Priya’s paintings are currently included. Art and gathering enquiry categories represent interests or potential requests, not evidence of completed commissions, hosted events, awards or testimonials.

## Four-place scroll journey

The `priya-nyc.webp`, `priya-hike.webp` and `priya-winter.webp` assets are generated attire variants of the supplied character reference. `winter-studio.webp` is an imagined creative interior with a snowy view, not a photograph of Priya’s real home. The hero transitions among these illustrations with native scrolling; the underlying identity reference and original generated images remain available in the project. Prompts are in `ASSET-PROMPTS.txt`.

## Artist and moving companion

`priya-artist.webp` is a built-in imagegen identity-preserving edit: camera and straps were replaced with a brush and palette. The small walking companion, pine grove and birds in `companion.js` are original articulated SVG illustrations with CSS motion. They are decorative characters, not photographs. Generated walking sheets failed motion quality checks and were not included.
